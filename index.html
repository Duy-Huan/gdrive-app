Personal Google Drive Sync

A personal-use OAuth client configuration for rclone on Linux. It allows the owner to authenticate their own Google Drive account to browse, mount, and synchronize Drive files using rclone.

What this integration does

The owner explicitly operates rclone to read and manage Drive data. Depending on the configured OAuth scope, rclone may list, read, upload, modify or delete Drive files. This informational website does not collect Google user information or handle OAuth redirects.

Privacy & terms

Review the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for information on data access, credential storage, and responsible usage.

Intended audience

This OAuth setup is for the account owner's personal devices, not a publicly offered file-management service.
